The Author's Take · Opinion
My opinion, plainly labeled as opinion: do not wait for a wave of St. Andrews inventory. A home-sale tax exclusion that has not moved since 1997, a Save Our Homes cap that rewards staying, and an Amendment 3 exemption that grows in 2027 and 2028 all push owners to hold. Homes will come to market one life chapter at a time, often with little notice. Buyers who are ready, and who tell me what they want before it is public, will get the best ones.
