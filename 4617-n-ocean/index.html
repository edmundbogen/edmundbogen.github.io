Diligence

What has to be verified before this is real

Ranked by how much damage each one does if the assumption fails. The first two can move value by seven figures.

CriticalThe unincorporated status is the entire thesis — and it is unverified
Every dollar of STR premium in this price rests on one claim: that the parcel sits in unincorporated Palm Beach County rather than Gulf Stream, Briny Breezes, or Ocean Ridge. The 4600 block of N Ocean Blvd sits in the seam between those jurisdictions — Briny Breezes town hall is at 5000 N Ocean, Gulf Stream runs to the south, and third-party records label this parcel's area "Gulf Stream." Pull the parcel from the Palm Beach County Property Appraiser and get written zoning confirmation from PBC Planning & Zoning before spending another hour on this. If it falls inside a municipality, the nightly-rental income disappears and the value with it.
CriticalNo T-12, no rent roll, no operating statements
The package is a photo gallery and a renovation list. There is no trailing twelve, no rent roll, no leases, no STR booking history, no tax bill, no insurance declarations page, no utility statements. You are being asked to price a 9-unit income property entirely on adjectives. Nothing in this model is the seller's number — every figure is a market estimate. Request the full financial package in writing and treat continued silence as information.
HighProperty taxes reset to your purchase price
Florida reassesses on transfer, and non-homestead investment property has no Save Our Homes cap. At roughly 1.9% of a $3.28M basis that is about $62,000 a year. If the seller has held long, their current bill could be a third of that — and any cap rate quoted off their historical tax line is fiction. Model it at your basis, not theirs.
HighInsurance on a nine-unit coastal building 175 yards from the ocean
Impact windows and new stucco help. Construction type, roof age, and elevation drive the rest. Coastal frame stock can carry premiums that erase a full point of cap rate on their own, and Flood Zone AE or VE exposure sits on top of the wind policy. Get three real quotes with the property schedule and loss runs before you go hard on deposit — not an agent's estimate.
HighSeptic on a nine-unit income property
The listing calls the septic system "updated," which is not the same as permitted for nine units of density including four high-turnover nightly rentals. Confirm the permitted capacity with the Florida Department of Health in Palm Beach County, pull the permit history, and ask whether a sewer connection is available or mandated. Palm Beach County has been pushing coastal septic-to-sewer conversion; an assessment landing post-close is a five- or six-figure surprise.
VerifyThree different agents are attached to this listing
The email came from Gabriella Matire at Coldwell Banker, but the branded property tour lists Zachary Mazur (Coldwell Banker Main Group) and Josh Weinraub (Luxury Collective) as the contacts. Confirm who actually holds the listing agreement and what the co-broke is before you invest time. Not a deal issue — a get-it-in-writing issue.
VerifyTax layer sits on top of nightly revenue, not inside it
Palm Beach County Tourist Development Tax is 6% on stays of six months or less, on top of 6.5% state and county sales tax — 12.5% combined, and it applies to mandatory cleaning and pet fees too. Platforms collect much of this in Florida, but the registration, remittance and audit obligation is the owner's. Confirm who remits what before you underwrite gross revenue.
VerifyLegal unit count and certificates of occupancy
Nine units, three buildings, 10–11 parking spaces. Confirm all nine units are legally permitted and reflected in the property record — not converted or added without permit — and that parking satisfies the zoning requirement for the use. Unpermitted units are unfinanceable and uninsurable, and lenders check.
