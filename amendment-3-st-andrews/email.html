Amendment 3 Passed. Here’s What It Means for St. Andrews.

Amendment 3 passed with about 62 percent of the vote. We ran the numbers on a theoretical $3 million St. Andrews home bought three years ago: by 2028 its tax bill comes in about $2,178 a year lower than it would have under the old rules. The bigger story is the calendar. People who are Florida residents by December 31, 2026 get the full exemption, while those who arrive later wait four years. The Author's Take · Opinion
My opinion, plainly labeled as opinion: Amendment 3 is a tailwind for St. Andrews values, not a rocket. The savings on a home like ours are modest in dollars, but they are permanent, they grow with inflation after 2029, and they reward owners who stay. That keeps inventory inside the gates tight. Add a December 31 residency date that is worth close to $7,700 in avoided taxes on a home like our example, and relocating buyers have a real reason to make their move this year. I expect the next eight weeks to be busier than a typical holiday season.
