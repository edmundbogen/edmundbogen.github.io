The Country Says Buyer's Market. Palm Beach County Says Otherwise.

Redfin counted a record-low pool of American homebuyers in July and called nearly 80% of major metros buyer's markets. Both readings are real — this week Edmund breaks down why they diverge, what his own MLS data shows, and the one segment where the national story actually applies here.
