How Palm Beach County Stacks Up Against the World’s Great Luxury Markets

I compared our county with Manhattan, the Hamptons, Greenwich, Miami and Aspen: record sales at the top, a five-year prime price gain that outpaced Miami, Aspen, the Hamptons and New York in Knight Frank’s index, and a few honest soft spots. Here is how well Palm Beach County is positioned, and what it means if you are buying.
By Edmund Bogen · November 16, 2026 · Written with the assistance of artificial intelligence — every figure sourced below

I came to South Florida from New York about thirteen years ago, and I have spent every one of those years selling homes here. A question I hear more than any other, often from buyers who already own in Manhattan, the Hamptons, Greenwich or California, is a simple one: is Palm Beach County the real thing, or just a moment? So this week I set the stories aside and pulled the numbers. I compared our county with the other great luxury markets in America and around the world, and I want to share honestly what I found, the good and the not so good, because if you are buying here you deserve both.

The top of our market just had its best year on record

I pulled every residential closing of $10 million or more in Palm Beach County from BeachesMLS. From January 1 through November 15 there were 201 of them this year, against 138 in the same stretch of 2025 and 112 in 2024. Sales of $5 million or more rose to 582, from 449 a year ago. With six weeks still to go, 2026 has already passed the 160 closings of $10 million or more that the MLS recorded for all of 2025.

Others see the same thing. MIAMI REALTORS reported that the county passed its old annual record of 169 sales of $10 million or more by the end of August, and that 21 sales of $30 million or more through September had already tied all of 2025. On the island itself, Corcoran counted 29 single-family sales in the third quarter, up 190 percent from a year earlier, at a median of $12.0 million, up 20 percent. And for the first three quarters of the year, the price it takes to reach the top 5 percent of single-family sales was higher in Palm Beach County, at $4.3 million, than in any other South Florida county, Miami-Dade included.

How the other great markets are doing

Here is where it gets interesting. Manhattan’s median sale price held at a record $1.25 million in the third quarter, up 5.9 percent, according to appraiser Jonathan Miller. Greenwich’s median rose 17 percent to $3.0 million, according to Sotheby’s International Realty, though sales fell 8 percent and listings rose. Miami’s beach communities had a strong quarter too, with a single-family median of $3.3 million, up 17 percent, according to Corcoran. The Hamptons went the other way: the median fell 6.3 percent to $1,875,000, and the number of sales has now dropped year over year for four quarters in a row.

The longer view is the one that moved me. Knight Frank’s Wealth Report 2026 tracks prime home prices in 100 of the world’s most expensive markets. Over the five years through 2025, Palm Beach rose 90.5 percent. Miami rose 67.1 percent, Aspen 57.7 percent, the Hamptons 52.2 percent and New York 3.1 percent.

Palm Beach County is no longer the place you visit between seasons somewhere else. For more and more buyers, it is the first choice.

Why the demand keeps coming

Some of it is business. In January, Wells Fargo said it would move its wealth management headquarters to One Flagler in West Palm Beach, joining tenants such as Goldman Sachs, BlackRock, JPMorgan and Point72 in the same building. When firms like these plant a flag here, the people who run them tend to want to live nearby.

Some of it is the tax map. This month California voters rejected Proposition 40, a one-time 5 percent tax on wealth above $1 billion, by 52.2 to 47.8 percent in the count so far. New York did not raise income taxes this year, but its budget added a new surcharge on New York City second homes worth $5 million or more. Here at home, Florida voters passed Amendment 3, which raises the homestead exemption from non-school property taxes to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028.

And some of it is conviction. In September, more than half of the county’s closed sales were paid in cash, according to MIAMI REALTORS. Buyers are choosing to be here, and many of them are not waiting on a lender to do it.

The honest caveats

Not every corner of our market is rising. In September, Palm Beach County had 6.8 months of condominium inventory, which MIAMI REALTORS calls balanced, and the typical condo sold for 93 percent of its original asking price. Total county sales that month were actually down 3.3 percent from a year earlier; the strength is in dollar volume and at the top. Between Hypoluxo Island and Highland Beach, Corcoran found single-family listings up 51 percent in the third quarter.

At the very top there is real choice. Today 287 homes in the county are listed at $10 million or more. At this year’s pace of sales, that is roughly 15 months of supply by my own math. Single-family homes overall are a different story: 3.6 months of supply, a seller’s market, with the typical house closing at 95 percent of its original asking price.

What this means for you as a buyer

Closer to home, St. Andrews Country Club has 11 homes for sale this week, from $2.5 million to $19.975 million, and 31 closed sales so far this year. Across Boca Raton, the median residential sale price in October was $675,000, up 8 percent from a year earlier, while the number of homes for sale fell about 22 percent, according to BeachesMLS.

My opinion, and it is only opinion: Palm Beach County is as well positioned as any luxury market in the country right now. That does not mean every home is priced right. In a tight single-family market the well-priced homes still go first, so decide what you want before it comes to market. At $10 million and up, and in condominiums, you have more room to negotiate than you have had in years. And if this will be your primary home, ask your tax adviser how Amendment 3’s timing applies to you, because the date you establish Florida residency may matter.

Thirteen years ago I bet my career on this place. The numbers tell me I was not the only one who saw it. If you are thinking about joining us, or moving up within the county, I would be glad to walk you through it, quietly and with no obligation.
