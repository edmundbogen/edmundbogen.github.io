Myth vs. Market
Myth: a presidential footprint nearby instantly lifts every home value.
Market: Palm Beach $10M-plus contracts did jump 400% year-over-year after the 2024 election, per Luxury Tribune — but no controlled study separates that from Florida's tax draw and thin inventory. What is measurable is where the costs land: shifted flight paths near Mar-a-Lago now affect roughly 11,000 homes, all within a tight radius. Distance decides which side of the trade you are on.
