Living Longer in the Sunshine

Americans are living longer than ever — and Palm Beach County residents longer still. What the longevity boom means for how we plan, how we buy, and what a home is really for.

The CDC's final 2024 numbers are in: U.S. life expectancy hit a record 79.0 years, and a 65-year-old can now expect nearly 20 more — meaning retirement is a two-to-three-decade chapter, not a ten-year wind-down. Palm Beach County runs at roughly 80.7 years, well above the state and national averages. This week I look at the record data, the $12.5 trillion "longevity economy," why 75% of adults over 50 want to age in place, the 60-second legal explainer behind "55+" communities — and why the right home has quietly become a longevity asset.
