SWITCH GUIDE

How to use it

Photograph each bank of light switches, tap each switch, and say what it controls. You get a polished Home Guide for your buyer, a one-page Showing Sheet, and a QR card to leave in the house.

1 Put it on your phone

Open the link in Safari on iPhone (or Chrome on Android). Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

From then on, always open Switch Guide from that icon. It works inside the house even with no signal. (Your properties live in the icon’s copy, not in the Safari tab.)

2 Set up your profile

The first time, enter your name, brokerage, license number, phone, email, headshot, logo, and a color. They appear on every guide. You can change them any time under My profile & branding.

3 Start a property

Tap New property and type the address. Add a cover photo of the home if you like. It goes on the front of the Home Guide.

4 Walk the house

  • Tap Add room, starting with the first room you walk into.
  • Tap Add switch bank and photograph the wall plate straight on, filling the screen.
  • Tap a switch in the photo. A number appears. Type what it controls (“Foyer chandelier”) and tap Return. Then tap the next switch.
  • Short on time? Tap one of the suggestions instead of typing.
  • Number in the wrong spot? Drag it onto the switch.

5 Mark the showing lights

Tap the ☆ next to any switch that should be turned on for showings. Details lets you add the type of switch (dimmer, fan, shades…) and a short note such as “hold to dim.”

6 Put rooms in walking order

On the property screen, press and drag the ≡ handle to arrange rooms (and the switch banks inside each room) in the order you walk through the house. The guides follow this order.

7 Share it

  • Home Guide: the closing gift, with every room, photo, and numbered list.
  • Showing Sheet: one or two pages listing only the ☆ switches, in walking order.
  • QR card: upload the Home Guide to Google Drive or Dropbox, set sharing to “anyone with the link,” paste the link, and print. Cut out the cards and leave one inside a kitchen cabinet and one by the electrical panel.

When a file is ready, tap Share or Save to save it to Files, AirDrop it, or send it by email or text.

8 Back up after every visit

Your work is stored only on your phone. Nobody else has a copy. Tap Back up and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself. The app reminds you when a property has changes that aren’t backed up.

To move a property to your laptop or hand it to an assistant, send them the file. They tap Import a backup file.

Never put codes or passwords in a guide. The guide may be printed and left in the house. Don’t include alarm, gate, garage, or door codes or Wi-Fi passwords. The app warns you if something looks like one.

When the app says Update available, tap Update now. Nothing you’ve entered is lost. The guide is a convenience reference to light switches. It is not electrical documentation and has not been checked by an electrician. · Switch Guide by Edmund Bogen
